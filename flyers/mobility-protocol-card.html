MOBILITY WITH PILATES — Session Protocol

The Base × Santa Barbara Pilates  ·  60 min
Block 1 · 20 min
Core, Hips & Glutes
Circuit 1 — Hips · 2 rounds
  1. 1
    Banded lateral walks — 10 steps each way
  2. 2
    Side-lying clams — 12/side
  3. 3
    Banded leg raises → 6 clam pulses — 10/side
  4. 4
    Bird dogs — 8/side · slow · 2-sec hold
Circuit 2 — Core & Posterior · 2 rounds
  1. 5
    Mountain climbers — 20 total · controlled
  2. 6
    Front plank → shoulder taps — 30 sec + 10 taps
  3. 7
    Back extension reachers → Y-T flys — 8 + 8
  4. 8
    Kneeling thoracic twists — 8/side
Band goes on at station 1 and stays on through the circuit. Keep transitions under 30 seconds — the pace is the product.
