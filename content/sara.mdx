## Training

I completed my BASI Comprehensive certification in 2008. Since then I have continued to study — Polestar Rehabilitation, mentorship with many of the great Pilates teachers here in Santa Barbara, and a habit of taking sessions as a student two or three times a month. The work asks for a teacher who is still learning.

## The studio

I opened Santa Barbara Pilates in 2026, after fifteen years of teaching elsewhere in town. I wanted a small place. A place where the apparatus is in good condition, the room is quiet, and an instructor has time to watch your hip move and tell you what they see.

We are adjacent to The Base Gym, but we are our own studio. Different entrance. Different room. Different idea about what an hour of training is for.

## How I teach

Directly. I am confident in the work and clear in my corrections — I would rather adjust you with a hand on your ribs than a sentence across the room. Pilates is taught by what the instructor notices, not by what they say.

I teach privates myself, every week. I also train and mentor every instructor who joins the studio, and I sit in on their early sessions until I know how they teach.

## Outside the studio

I live in Santa Barbara with my husband and our daughter. I surf, I dance, I practice yoga, meditation, and Reiki. I read more than I watch.

If you would like to begin, write to me. A first private session is the easiest way to know whether this work is for you.
